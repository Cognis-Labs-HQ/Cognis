# Sharing

Share supported content with selected people or through a public link while controlling their access.

## What You Can Do

Available actions are shown on [the feature page](/) according to your account permissions.

## Before You Start

Check that Sharing is enabled and that your account has permission for the operation.

## Get Started

Open [the feature](/) and choose the action you need.

[Usage](/docs/gateways/share/usage) explains procedures. [Technical](/docs/gateways/share/technical) documents interfaces and configuration.
