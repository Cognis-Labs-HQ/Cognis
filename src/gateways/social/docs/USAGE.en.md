# Usage

Practical procedures for Social Features.

## Prerequisites

Sign in; individual social adapters supply their own features.

## Examples

Visit a permitted profile, follow or message through available controls, and check the resulting relationship or room.

## Expected Results

Social operations use current identity and access checks; disabling one adapter removes its contributions.

## Troubleshooting

Check enabled adapters, blocks and membership. The gateway alone does not supply every social feature.
