# Usage

Practical procedures for Calendar.

## Prerequisites

Open Calendar with your account or a valid shared-calendar link. Event changes require ownership or granted edit access.

## Examples

Add an event with its date and time, save and switch between available date views to check its placement.

In a grid, choose a month title in Year to open that month, a week number to open its week, or a day indicator to open that date. These navigation controls also work in read-only shared calendars.

## Expected Results

Saved events appear in the selected calendar; narrow screens use a readable agenda while wide screens can use grids.

## Troubleshooting

Check the selected calendar, period and timezone. Shared access and offline queuing retain their separate permission and delivery rules.
