# Profiles and Posts

Introduce yourself, publish posts and choose whom to follow or block.

## What You Can Do

Edit your profile, publish posts and manage social visibility and relationships.

## Before You Start

Sign in to edit your own profile; other profiles respect their privacy and blocking rules.

## Get Started

Open your avatar to visit your profile and choose an available edit or post action.
