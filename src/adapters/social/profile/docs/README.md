# Profiles and Posts

Introduce yourself, publish posts and choose whom to follow or block.

## What You Can Do

- Review the profile identity and availability; your own profile provides editing controls.
- Browse visible posts and use the post actions allowed for your account.
- Follow, message or manage relationships from the profile controls; review the selected person before acting.

## Before You Start

Check that Profiles and Posts is enabled and that your account has permission for the operation.

## Get Started

Open [the feature](/profile) and choose the action you need.
