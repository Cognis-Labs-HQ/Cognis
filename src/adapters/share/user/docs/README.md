# Share with People

Give selected Cognis users access to content without publishing a public link.

## What You Can Do

Available actions are shown on [the feature page](/) according to your account permissions.

## Before You Start

Check that Share with People is enabled and that your account has permission for the operation.

## Get Started

Open [the feature](/) and choose the action you need.

[Usage](/docs/adapters/share/user/usage) explains procedures. [Technical](/docs/adapters/share/user/technical) documents interfaces and configuration.
