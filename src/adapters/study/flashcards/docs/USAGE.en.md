# Usage

Practical procedures for Flashcards.

## Prerequisites

Sign in and select an enrolled language; editing and exports require the appropriate deck access.

## Examples

Filter decks, choose reading or writing, complete cards and review feedback; use the favourite control to keep useful decks nearby.

Open a visible deck from Flashcards to begin practicing immediately. Use Total, Done, Due or New to choose the cards to study.

## Expected Results

Completed trusted practice records progress and eligible rewards. Deck edits persist only after saving.

## Troubleshooting

Empty offline lists need prepared enrolled-language data. Shared access does not grant creator-only deck export rights.
